# Adoption receipts

A design partner "running the full stack" is a claim. This template turns
it into evidence. One copy per partner; fill it in from their repo's
committed record, not from memory. Every row names the artifact that
proves it — if there's no artifact, the row stays open.

The launch's public claims stay within what these receipts support.

## Partner

- **Who:** (name / handle, and the repo or project, if shareable)
- **Context:** (solo or team, editor: Cursor / Claude Code / both, prior
  workflow)
- **Started:** (date the kit was bootstrapped)

## Receipts

| # | Claim | Artifact that proves it | Status |
|---|-------|------------------------|--------|
| 1 | Bootstrap succeeded | `house-rules doctor` output showing `SUMMARY: ok` on their repo | open |
| 2 | Real work closed with evidence | A bead close reason from their tracker with AC-mapped evidence and a commit ref (`close-reason-lint` passes on it) | open |
| 3 | A checker used operationally | A commit, CI log, or session record where `defer-lint`, `banned-token-scan`, `close-reason-lint`, or `tdd-ledger verify` caught something or gated something | open |
| 4 | Rules synced or upgraded | A sync commit in their repo after a kit update (`house-rules sync` diff, or a version-pin change) | open |
| 5 | Retained by choice | After 2+ weeks of real work: still installed, and the partner says why — verbatim quote, including reservations | open |

## What went wrong

The most valuable section. Friction, confusing errors, rules the agent
ignored, checks that fired wrongly, anything they worked around. Each item
here becomes an issue or a bead — record the ID once filed.

## Verbatim reactions

Quotes only, attributed and dated, kept whole. No paraphrasing — a
paraphrased quote is a fabricated one. Ask permission before using any
quote publicly.
